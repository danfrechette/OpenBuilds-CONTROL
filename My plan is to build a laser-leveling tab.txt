Laser Leveling Table Controller Project
Project Overview

The goal of this project is to develop a standalone laser-leveling table that mounts beneath an existing laser cutter. The system will utilize a BLTouch probe to measure the orientation of the table's Z-plane and identify any unevenness across the work surface.

Independent stepper motors will automatically adjust the position of the workpiece, allowing the table to be leveled prior to operation. This capability is intended to improve cutting consistency and compensate for variations in material thickness or table alignment.

To enhance reliability and protect the equipment, the system should also be capable of detecting stepper motor stalls. Stall detection will be used during the leveling process and as a safeguard against mechanical obstructions or bed damage.

The controller board will function as the central processing unit for the entire system, managing the leveling mechanism as well as auxiliary devices such as lighting, cooling fans, and powered USB accessories.

The design should support operation from either a 12V or 24V power source. Voltage selection should be configurable through onboard DIP switches or jumpers. USB-powered devices should also support configurable output voltages ranging from 5V to 24V, providing flexibility for a variety of accessories.

An OLED display will provide status information, configuration menus, leveling feedback, error messages, and system diagnostics.

The system is intended to operate independently and will not directly interface with the laser cutter's motion controller, firmware, or control electronics. Its sole purpose is bed leveling, workpiece alignment, and auxiliary device control.

To allow future expansion, additional I/O ports should be included for optional limit switches and other accessories.
